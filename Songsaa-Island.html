A Frontier of Coastal Luxury

Where Pristine Coral Meets
Sovereign Tropical Design

Nestled across two intimate, untouched islands in Cambodia's sapphire Koh Rong Archipelago, Song Saa Private Island effortlessly redefines the peak of sustainable island luxury. Conceived with deep respect for the environment, this award-winning destination stands as a sanctuary where premium hospitality actively protects thriving marine life, sprawling coral reefs, and native rainforest paths.

Your journey is smooth and enchanting: transition smoothly from Sihanoukville via a scenic premium speedboat voyage, stepping directly onto sugar-soft white sands and shimmering boardwalks. The estate encompasses a collection of ultra-private customized villas—including dramatic overwater sanctuaries and hidden jungle hideaways—each boasting a private pool and sweeping ocean horizons.

Dine on sustainably caught seafood and organic seasonal produce at the overwater Vista Restaurant, sample custom elixirs at the ocean-breeze Drift Bar, or unwind in the beautiful main infinity pool seamlessly blending with the gulf. Beyond standard hospitality, your presence directly supports the Song Saa Foundation's marine preservation squads, fusing indulgent relaxation with meaningful ecological purpose.

Location & Getting There

A Protected Marine Enclave, Meticulously Connected

Song Saa Private Island sits across two private gems within the pristine Koh Rong Archipelago, roughly 45 minutes by speedboat from the Sihanoukville coast. Safely secluded, the island offers complete coastal immersion alongside smooth, high-end private transfers.

Sihanoukville International Airport (KOS) 30-45 minutes private luxury car transfer to the port
Sihanoukville Main Luxury Pier 45 minutes by comfortable private luxury speedboat transfer
Phnom Penh International Airport (PNH) Approx 2.5 hours via the new expressway to the coast
Song Saa Marine Reserve Perimeter Surrounds the resort island grounds completely
