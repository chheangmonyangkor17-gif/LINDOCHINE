Indochina Full-Detail Culinary Journey

The Experience

Multi Country Culinary Journeys

Prepare your senses for an unforgettable 17-day epicurean adventure through the heart of Southeast Asia. This meticulously crafted itinerary transcends a typical vacation, offering a deep, sensory exploration of four of the world's most vibrant culinary cultures. From the fiery street food of Bangkok to the subtle, aromatic dishes of Cambodia, you will not only taste iconic cuisines but also immerse yourself in their traditions through exclusive, hands-on cooking classes, private market visits, and authentic dining experiences.

Duration17 Days / 16 Nights
ThemeCulinary Journeys
LocationBangkok, Chaing Mai, Luang Prabang, Siem Reap, Ha noi, Hoi An
Country Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, Cambodia

Route: Bangkok → Chaing Mai → Luang Prabang → Siem Reap → Ha noi → Hoi an

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival & Street Food Immersion

LOCATIONBangkok

ACTIVITIES Upon arrival at Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), you will be met by a private driver and transferred to the legendary Mandarin Oriental, Bangkok. After checking in and enjoying a brief rest, the evening is dedicated to an expert-guided street food tour. Your guide will lead you through the vibrant and illuminated streets of Chinatown (Yaowarat), a bustling market of culinary wonders. You will sample iconic dishes like the perfectly balanced Pad Thai, a fragrant and spicy Tom Yum Goong (hot and sour shrimp soup), and sweet, creamy Mango Sticky Rice from a renowned vendor.

ACCOMMODATION Mandarin Oriental Bangkok

Dinner Michelin Street Food: Crab Omelette at Jay Fai or Yaowarat Classics

Day 2: Flavors & Fablesy

LOCATION Bangkok

ACTIVITIES The day begins with a private long-tail boat tour along Bangkok's scenic khlongs (canals). This unique perspective allows you to see a more traditional side of the city, with teak houses and floating markets. In the afternoon, you will participate in an exclusive hands-on Thai cooking class. Under the guidance of a professional chef, you will learn to prepare traditional curries from scratch, making your own curry paste using a mortar and pestle, and master the art of balancing the five fundamental Thai tastes.

ACCOMMODATION Mandarin Oriental Bangkok

Breakfast Riverside Sunrise: International Buffet at The Verandah
Lunch Chef's Table: Savor Your Handcrafted Thai Creations
Dinner Chao Phraya Elegance: Fine Dining at Le Normandie

Day 3: Northern Thai Cooking

LOCATION Chaing Mai

ACTIVITIES After a final Thai breakfast, a morning flight takes you to Chiang Mai (CNX). Upon arrival, you'll be transferred to the exquisite 137 Pillars House. After checking in, your guide will take you to a local market to discover unique Northern Thai ingredients. Your dinner is a taste of Northern Thai specialties at a celebrated local restaurant, featuring the creamy coconut curry noodle soup Khao Soi and the aromatic, grilled pork sausage Sai Oua.

ACCOMMODATION 137 Pillars House

Dinner Lanna Heritage: Khao Soi & Northern Thai Specialties

Day 4: Culinary Immersion in Chiang Mai

LOCATION Chiang Mai

ACTIVITIES The day is dedicated to a full-day, hands-on cooking class in a beautiful countryside setting. The experience begins with a visit to an organic farm where you will hand-pick fresh vegetables and herbs for your dishes. Your class, led by a local family chef, will teach you how to prepare authentic Northern Thai dishes and make your own curry paste. You will then sit down to enjoy the feast you prepared.

ACCOMMODATION 137 Pillars House

Lunch Farm-to-Fork: Self-Prepared Organic Thai Feast
Dinner Lanna Heritage: Traditional Khantoke Tasting Menu

Day 5: The Slow Pace of Luang Prabang

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES After breakfast, you will fly from Chiang Mai to Luang Prabang (LPQ). Upon arrival, you will be transferred to your hotel. The rest of the day is at your leisure to explore this enchanting town on foot. In the evening, stroll through the vibrant night market and sample local street food, including the famous all-you-can-eat vegetarian buffet.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Luang Prabang

Dinner Market Flavors: Street Food Safari & Vegetarian Buffet

Day 6: Authentic Lao Flavors

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Rise early to observe the daily alms giving ceremony, a silent and serene tradition. Afterwards, take a guided tour of the local morning market to see the ingredients for traditional Lao dishes. Later, you will take a cooking class at the well-known Tamarind Cooking School, learning to prepare the national dish Laap (a savory and spicy minced meat salad) and Tam Mak Hoong (Lao papaya salad).

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Luang Prabang

Lunch Culinary Creation: Handmade Laap & Papaya Salad
Dinner Lao Fine Dining: Traditional Heritage Menu at The Great House

Day 7: Khmer Cuisine in Siem Reap

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIESEnjoy a final breakfast in Laos before a flight to Siem Reap (SAI). Upon arrival, transfer to the iconic Raffles Grand Hotel d'Angkor. Your introduction to Cambodian cuisine begins with a dinner at a fine dining restaurant, tasting the nuanced flavors of classic dishes like Amok (a creamy and delicate fish curry) and the zesty Beef Lok Lak.

ACCOMMODATION Raffles Grand Hotel D'angkor

Lunch Riverside Breakfast: International Buffet at Sofitel
Dinner Khmer Classics: Traditional Fish Amok & Beef Lok Lak

Day 8: Market & Masterclass

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIES Begin your day with an expert-guided tour of the Old Market (Phsar Chas). You will learn about the unique herbs and spices that define Cambodian cuisine, particularly the various types of kroeung paste. In the afternoon, a cooking class will teach you how to prepare your own kroeung from scratch and use it in a traditional Khmer dish.

ACCOMMODATION Raffles Grand Hotel D'angkor

Lunch Market Discoveries: Seasonal Khmer Street Flavors
Dinner Masterclass Feast: Hand-Pounded Kroeung & Khmer Curries

Day 9: Countryside & Cuisine

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIES Take a private tuk-tuk tour to the Cambodian countryside. You'll visit a rural village to see how families live and eat. The tour includes a visit to a rice paper making workshop or a traditional noodle maker. You will also enjoy a home-cooked lunch with a local family, providing an authentic glimpse into Cambodian life and food.

ACCOMMODATION Raffles Grand Hotel D'angkor

Lunch Village Table: Authentic Home-Cooked Khmer Exchange
Dinner Market to Plate: Contemporary Khmer Dining in Siem Reap

Day 10: Hanoi - An Urban Culinary Adventure

LOCATIONHanoi

ACTIVITIES After a final breakfast, fly from Siem Reap to Hanoi (HAN). Check into your hotel. In the afternoon, you will embark on a guided street food tour of Hanoi's Old Quarter. Your guide will introduce you to street stalls serving Pho, the iconic noodle soup, Bun Cha (grilled pork with noodles), and the famous egg coffee.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Legend Metropole

Dinner Old Quarter Safari: Bun Cha, Pho & Egg Coffee Tasting

Day 11: Culinary & Cultural Icons

LOCATIONHanoi

ACTIVITIES Start your day with a classic Hanoi breakfast of Pho Bo (beef noodle soup). Afterwards, visit the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum and the Temple of Literature. In the evening, enjoy a meal at a restaurant known for its exquisite Vietnamese fine dining, a contrast to the street food experience.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Legend Metropole

Breakfast Hanoi Essential: Traditional Pho Bo Morning Ritual
Dinner Indochine Elegance: Exquisite Vietnamese Fine Dining

Day 12: Journey to Hoi An

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIES fly from Hanoi to Da Nang (DAD) and transfer to the charming town of Hoi An. The evening is at your leisure to explore the lantern-lit streets. Enjoy dinner and a relaxing lantern-making workshop, a signature activity of Hoi An.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Breakfast Farewell Hanoi: International Buffet at your Hotel
Dinner Riverside Charm: Contemporary Vietnamese Dining at Hoi An Riverside

Day 13: Basket Boats & Culinary Secrets

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIESYour day begins with a cycling tour to a rural village. From there, you will take a basket boat ride through the coconut groves to your cooking school. The class will teach you how to prepare dishes unique to the region like Cao Lầu (a noodle dish unique to Hoi An) and Banh Xeo (savory pancakes).

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Breakfast Riverside Start: Al-Fresco Breakfast at Lanterns
Lunch Chef's Table: Your Handmade Cao Lầu & Banh Xeo Feast
Dinner Heritage Flavors: Fine Dining in the Heart of Ancient Town

Day 14: The Best Banh Mi

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIES A morning food tour is dedicated to finding the best Banh Mi in Vietnam. You'll visit several acclaimed vendors and decide on your favorite. The afternoon is at your leisure to explore Hoi An's ancient town, its tailors, and its many shops.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Breakfast Gourmet Start: Riverside Buffet at Anantara
Lunch The Ultimate Quest: Banh Mi Tasting & Street Food Tour
Dinner Riverside Charm: Contemporary Vietnamese Dining at Hoi An Riverside

Day 15: Central Vietnamese Delights

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final delicious breakfast in Hoi An. In the afternoon, take a visit to the local Tra Que Vegetable Village, a short bike ride or taxi away. Here, you'll learn about the traditional farming methods used to grow the aromatic herbs that make Vietnamese food so special. For your final dinner, enjoy a multi-course tasting menu at a top restaurant in Hoi An.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Breakfast Riverside Morning: Gourmet Breakfast at Lanterns
Lunch Garden-to-Table: Organic Specialties at Tra Que Village
Dinner Modern Flavors: Multi-Course Tasting Menu at Hoi An Riverside

Day 16: Final Flavors & Departure Preparation

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final, leisurely morning in Hoi An. Take some time to revisit a favorite local spot for a last meal or coffee, or do some last-minute souvenir shopping. The rest of the day is at your leisure.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Breakfast Hoi An Farewell: International Buffet at Lanterns
Dinner The Grand Finale: Farewell Tasting Menu at Hoi An Riverside

Day 17: Departure

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIES Enjoy your final Vietnamese breakfast. You will be transferred to Da Nang Airport for your flight home, filled with incredible memories and new culinary skills.

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Breakfast Last Tastes of Vietnam: Final Breakfast at Lanterns
