7 Days / Laos

Laos: Serene Sanctuaries & Inner Harmony

The Experience

Laos wellness & Rejuvenation

This exquisitely curated journey is designed for profound wellness and rejuvenation, offering a tranquil escape amidst the spiritual beauty and pristine natural landscapes of Laos. Centered in the enchanting UNESCO World Heritage city of Luang Prabang, this itinerary focuses on holistic well-being, blending traditional Lao healing practices, mindful activities, healthy cuisine, and serene relaxation in unparalleled luxury settings. Reconnect with yourself, embrace inner harmony, and emerge revitalized.

Duration7 Days / 6 Nights
ThemeWellness & Rejuvenation
Location Luang Prabang
CountryLaos

Detailed Map Breakdown

Route: Luang Prabang

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival & Rosewood Sanctuary

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Upon your morning or afternoon arrival at Luang Prabang (LPQ) International Airport, a private luxury transfer will whisk you to the serene Rosewood Luang Prabang. Settle into your elegantly designed accommodation amidst the lush landscape, then begin your wellness journey in the late afternoon with a personalized wellness consultation at Sense, A Rosewood Spa, helping to tailor your subsequent spa experiences. Enjoy some leisure time exploring the resort's tranquil grounds or by the pool before indulging in a light and healthy welcome dinner at The Great House restaurant at Rosewood, featuring wholesome Laotian and Western-inspired dishes crafted from locally sourced ingredients.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Dinner Wholesome Lao-Western Fusion at The Great House

Day 2: Holistic Rejuvenation & Forest Immersion

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Start your day with a private yoga or meditation session overlooking the tranquil streams within the Rosewood Luang Prabang resort, led by an experienced instructor. Following this, experience deep rejuvenation with a bespoke "Sense Journey" spa ritual at Sense, A Rosewood Spa, choosing from authentic Laotian therapies designed to cleanse, replenish, and balance body, mind, and soul using locally harvested botanicals. In the afternoon, engage in a guided "Forest Bathing" (Shinrin-yoku) experience within the resort's natural surroundings or a nearby secluded area, focusing on mindful connection with nature. Conclude your day by enjoying a serene and healthy dinner at the resort, perhaps opting for a private, candle-lit setting by the waterfall for a truly unique ambiance.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Dinner Candle-lit Waterfall Wellness Dinner

Day 3: Mindful Traditions & Culinary Harmony

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Embrace the serene spirit of Luang Prabang starting with an optional early morning observation of the deeply spiritual Alms Giving Ceremony (Tak Bat) from a respectful vantage point. The morning continues with an immersion in traditional Lao healing through a private session featuring a traditional Lao herbal steam bath followed by a therapeutic Lao massage, focusing on pressure points and energy flow for profound relaxation. After a light and healthy lunch at the resort, the afternoon invites you to participate in a private, hands-on healthy Lao cooking class, where you'll learn to prepare nutritious local dishes using fresh, seasonal ingredients. Conclude your day with a quiet and contemplative dinner at a refined restaurant in Luang Prabang town.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Lunch Culinary Creation: Healthy Lao Cooking Class

Day 4: Transition to Eco-Wellness & Riverside Tranquility

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final leisurely breakfast and a last moment of tranquility at Rosewood Luang Prabang, perhaps indulging in a gentle Qigong session or a relaxing dip in the pool. Late morning, a private luxury transfer will seamlessly transport you from Rosewood to The NamKhan Resort, an eco-friendly sanctuary nestled by the Khan River. Upon arrival, check into your unique accommodation and take time to explore the resort's expansive organic farm, visit its friendly animals, or simply relax by one of the infinity pools overlooking the river. Conclude your day by savoring a delicious, farm-to-table dinner at The NamKhan Resort's "Roots" restaurant, featuring fresh produce directly from their organic gardens.

ACCOMMODATION The NamKhan Resort

Dinner Farm-to-Table Experience at Roots

Day 5: Nature Immersion & Jungle Spa Rejuvenation

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Today is dedicated to deep connection with nature and profound rejuvenation at The NamKhan Resort. Begin your morning by joining the resort's complimentary daily yoga or Qigong class in the outdoor pavilion overlooking the Nam Khan River. Following this, embark on a guided mindful nature walk or light trek through the resort's surrounding organic gardens and jungle trails. The afternoon invites you to indulge in a signature treatment at The Jungle Spa at The NamKhan Resort, experiencing unique therapies like a "Rebalance Ritual" or an invigorating "Namkhan Signature Ritual," utilizing natural elements for ultimate relaxation. Conclude your day with a peaceful dinner at the resort, followed by tranquil stargazing.

ACCOMMODATION The NamKhan Resort

Lunch Nourishing Organic Garden Lunch

Day 6: Deep Relaxation & Personal Reflection

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Today is dedicated to profound relaxation and self-discovery. Begin your morning with a private guided meditation session focusing on breathwork and inner peace, or an advanced yoga practice tailored to your level. Late morning, immerse yourself in further rejuvenation with a detoxifying herbal bath followed by deep relaxation at The Jungle Spa. The entire afternoon is dedicated to free time for personal reflection, journaling, reading, or simply enjoying the serene ambiance of The NamKhan Resort—a vital chance for self-care. Conclude your retreat with a special farewell healthy dinner at the resort, emphasizing fresh, organic ingredients and celebrating the harmony you've cultivated.

ACCOMMODATION The NamKhan Resort

Dinner Special Farewell Healthy Celebration Dinner

Day 7: Departure from Serene Laos

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at The NamKhan Resort, taking a final moment to absorb the tranquility of the river and natural surroundings, perhaps with a short, reflective walk. A private luxury transfer will then seamlessly take you to Luang Prabang (LPQ) International Airport for your onward international journey, ensuring a comfortable departure filled with a renewed sense of well-being, inner harmony, and cherished memories of Laos's serene sanctuaries.

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Breakfast Final Mindful Riverside Breakfast
