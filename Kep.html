Kep-Sur-Mer

The Riveting Riviera of Cambodia

Faded glamour meets an intentional coastal awakening

A journey to Kep unveils a nostalgic chapter of Cambodia's history, where the gentle waves of the Gulf of Thailand softly brush against a storied shoreline. Once the prestigious playground for the French and Khmer elite during the mid-20th century, this elegant seaside retreat captures a poetic contrast between abandoned modernist villas and ultra-luxury boutique hill resorts hidden in lush greenery.

Far removed from the pace of busy metropolises, Kep values a slower, quiet luxury lifestyle. Celebrated for its legendary Blue Crab culinary heritage, misty mountain paths, and golden sunsets behind island horizons, it serves as an artistic sanctuary—offering a highly sophisticated, restorative, and unforgettable coastal chapter to any curated Indochina itinerary.

The Curated Treasures of Kep

The Waterfront Crab Market

Immerse yourself in Cambodia's ultimate coastal culinary scene. Watch local women wade out into the shallow surf to retrieve handmade wooden cages filled with freshly caught blue crabs. Savor this local catch directly at an overwater stilted restaurant, stir-fried with fragrant green Kampot pepper berries straight from the vine.

Kep National Park Canopy Trail

Explore a protected evergreen mountain sanctuary rising right over the coast. Walk or ride an open-top vintage transport through an 8-kilometer loop trail shaded by ancient jungle canopies. Along the way, discover sweeping panoramic lookouts over the gulf, local pepper plantations, and distant Vietnamese islands.

Koh Tonsay Pristine Escape

Board a private wooden longtail boat for a short, scenic 20-minute cruise to Rabbit Island. Unplug on secluded, white-sand beaches lined with swaying coconut trees, far from modern development. Relax in a hammock, swim in warm tropical waters, and enjoy fresh, locally prepared beachside seafood barbecues.

New Khmer Architecture Heritage Trail

Take an exclusive, curated architectural walk through Kep's Golden Age. Explore forgotten 1950s and 60s avant-garde villas influenced by Vann Molyvann and Le Corbusier. These concrete structural landmarks offer a poetic, haunting look into the mid-century lifestyle of the coastal elite.

Phnom Chhngok Cave Sanctuary

Venture slightly inland toward the limestone karst hills to discover an amazing archaeological treasure. Climb past wild vines into Phnom Chhngok cave, which houses a remarkably preserved 7th-century brick Shiva temple from the Funan era, lit by natural, dramatic cave skylights.

Salt Fields & Organic Pepper Terroir

Trace the historic craft traditions of the region. Visit the reflective coastal salt fields where sea salt is harvested by hand using ancestral methods. Continue to a certified organic farm to see why Kampot pepper is prized by world chefs as the finest gourmet seasoning on earth.
