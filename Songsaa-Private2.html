Song Saa Private Island, An Untouched Marine Sanctuary

Song Saa Private Island mirrors the raw, breathtaking beauty of Cambodia's Koh Rong Archipelago, establishing a revolutionary luxury baseline that intertwines castaway seclusion with profound environmental stewardship. Spanning two pristine, jungle-clad islets affectionately known as the 'Sweethearts' in Khmer, this intimate resort is built entirely from sustainable and repurposed local materials, flawlessly breathing refined luxury into driftwood, native stone, and salvaged fishing boats. Guests arrive via a scenic speedboat escape from Sihanoukville, stepping onto a sanctuary where overwater, jungle, and ocean-view villas await. Each standalone pavilion boasts its own private plunge pool, outdoor rain shower, and unobstructed vistas of the glittering, sapphire Gulf of Thailand. Dining here is a beautifully sustainable, culinary celebration of the senses; hyper-local seasonal ingredients and fresh, responsibly caught seafood are served over the water or directly onto the powdery sands. Complemented by the open-air Song Saa Sanctuaries, wellness takes a deeply spiritual turn, integrating bio-marine body wraps, traditional Khmer herb applications, and meditation techniques guided by the gentle rhythm of the ocean waves.

Style

Barefoot luxury and high-end eco-chic architecture utilizing reclaimed materials and traditional local thatched roofing.

Situation

Secluded across twin private tropical islands nestled within the protected waters of Cambodia's Koh Rong Archipelago.

Capacity

24 exceptionally private luxury villas, spanning intimate jungle configurations to vast overwater pavilions.

The Most

Cambodia's premier fully dynamic marine reserve, offering an extraordinary synthesis of isolation and ecological preservation.

The Experiments

  • Embark on a guided marine safari to explore the resort's private coral reef regeneration zone
  • Swim through an ethereal universe during a midnight ocean dip with glowing bioluminescent plankton
  • Kayak through pristine, ancient mangrove estuaries with a dedicated conservationist
  • Master vibrant local flavor profiles with a customized Khmer island cooking masterclass
  • Cleanse your spiritual energy with a blessing ritual guided by local Buddhist monks

The Extraordinary

Immerse yourself completely in the rhythms of the gulf with Song Saa's 'Destination Dining' signature experience. Escape to a completely deserted white sand bank or a starlit jetty platform jutting deep over the open ocean. Relish a bespoke, multi-course raw or grilled tasting menu meticulously prepared by your private chef, paired perfectly with vintage wines and the soft song of crashing island waves.

Ethics

Song Saa Private Island stands as a global pioneer of conservation luxury. Through the renowned Song Saa Foundation, the property launched Cambodia's first dedicated marine reserve, pioneered intensive artificial coral cultivation programs, and heavily supports adjacent village communities with comprehensive solid waste systems and local educational funding.
