01 — About Us

Company Information

L'Indochine is a luxury Destination Management Company (DMC) headquartered in Siem Reap, Kingdom of Cambodia. We specialise in designing and operating bespoke private journeys across Cambodia, Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, and multi-country Indochina itineraries for discerning leisure travellers and travel trade professionals.

References in these Terms to "L'Indochine," "we," "us," or "our" refer to the company and its authorised representatives. References to "you" or "the client" refer to the individual, business, or travel agent making a booking or enquiry.

02 — Bookings & Confirmations

How Your Journey is Confirmed

A booking is initiated when you submit a journey enquiry via our website, email, or through an authorised travel partner. L'Indochine will respond with a bespoke proposal detailing the itinerary, inclusions, pricing, and availability.

Your booking becomes confirmed only upon:

  • Written acceptance of the journey proposal by you or your authorised representative.
  • Receipt of the required deposit as specified in your proposal (typically 30–50% of the total journey cost).
  • Issuance of a written Booking Confirmation from L'Indochine.

L'Indochine reserves the right to decline or cancel any booking at its discretion prior to issuing a Booking Confirmation. No contract exists between us until all three conditions above are satisfied.

"Every journey we create is singular — curated to your story. Our confirmation process ensures that every detail is agreed upon before your adventure begins."

03 — Pricing & Payments

Rates, Currency & Payment Terms

All prices quoted are in US Dollars (USD) unless otherwise specified. Prices are valid for the dates indicated in your proposal and are subject to change prior to confirmation if costs from suppliers fluctuate significantly.

Our standard payment schedule is as follows:

  • Deposit: 30–50% of total journey value, due upon booking confirmation.
  • Balance: Remaining amount due no later than 45 days before the journey commencement date.
  • Short-lead bookings (confirmed within 45 days of departure): Full payment is required at confirmation.

Payments may be made via bank wire transfer, credit card (Visa, Mastercard), or through your travel agent. All bank transfer fees are the responsibility of the client. Credit card payments may be subject to a processing surcharge of up to 3%.

L'Indochine reserves the right to pass on any documented increases in government taxes, levies, or fuel surcharges that arise after booking confirmation. We will notify you promptly and in writing should such changes occur.

04 — Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation Policy

Should you need to cancel a confirmed booking, cancellation charges will apply based on the number of days prior to the journey commencement date on which we receive your written notice:

  • 90+ days before departure: Loss of deposit only.
  • 60–89 days before departure: 50% of total journey cost.
  • 30–59 days before departure: 75% of total journey cost.
  • Less than 30 days before departure: 100% of total journey cost — no refund.

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email to our reservations team. The date of receipt of your written cancellation will determine the applicable charge. Certain components — such as chartered flights, private vessels, or exclusive use bookings — may carry stricter cancellation terms, which will be clearly noted in your proposal.

We strongly recommend that all clients obtain comprehensive travel insurance, including coverage for cancellation, curtailment, medical expenses, and repatriation.

05 — Amendments & Changes

Modifying Your Journey

We understand that circumstances change. Amendment requests made after confirmation will be accommodated wherever possible, subject to supplier availability and any applicable re-booking fees. L'Indochine will notify you of any cost implications before proceeding with changes.

Significant amendments (changes to destination, dates, or number of travellers) made within 60 days of departure may be treated as a cancellation and rebooking, subject to the cancellation policy above.

L'Indochine reserves the right to make minor adjustments to your itinerary (e.g., reordering of activities, change of accommodation to an equivalent property) where necessary due to operational or safety reasons. We will always inform you promptly and strive to maintain the quality and spirit of your original journey.

06 — Travel Insurance

Insurance Requirements

Travel insurance is not included in our journey packages. We strongly advise — and for certain journey types require — that all travellers hold a comprehensive travel insurance policy before their departure. Your policy should include, at minimum:

  • Trip cancellation and curtailment.
  • Emergency medical treatment and hospitalisation.
  • Medical evacuation and repatriation.
  • Loss, theft, or damage of personal belongings.
  • Personal liability coverage.

L'Indochine shall not be liable for any costs, losses, or damages that would have been covered by an appropriate travel insurance policy. Details of your insurance policy must be provided to us prior to journey commencement upon request.

Entry Requirements & Health Advisories

It is your sole responsibility to hold a valid passport and to obtain all necessary visas, travel permits, and health documentation required for entry into each country on your itinerary. L'Indochine will provide general guidance on entry requirements upon request, but this information is advisory only and subject to change.

We recommend consulting your national government's official travel advisory portal and your healthcare provider well in advance of travel. Some destinations may require proof of vaccination or a negative health test result for entry.

L'Indochine accepts no liability for any client refused entry to a destination, detained, deported, or unable to travel due to failure to comply with passport, visa, health, or documentation requirements.
