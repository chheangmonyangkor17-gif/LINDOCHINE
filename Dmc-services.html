DMC Operational Excellence

Redefining Southeast Asia Ground Operations

At L'Indochine, our Destination Management Company (DMC) represents over a decade of proven expertise in Southeast Asia. We are more than logistics—we are architects of seamless travel experiences, orchestrating every detail with precision, innovation, and profound local knowledge.

Our strategic hub in Siem Reap, Cambodia positions us at the heart of Indochina, providing B2B partners with unparalleled ground handling, white-label solutions, and comprehensive destination management across Cambodia, Thailand, Laos, and Vietnam.

Our Services
Core Offering

DMC Operational Excellence

Complete destination management across the Indochina region. Our integrated services ensure flawless execution from initial inquiry to post-journey follow-up.

  • 24/7 On-Ground Support – Local teams across all destinations
  • Logistics Coordination – Flights, transfers, permits, guides
  • Accommodation Partnerships – Direct contracts with luxury properties
  • Activity & Experiences – Curated, exclusive offerings
  • Quality Assurance – Real-time monitoring and support

White-Label Ground Handling

  • Seamless Integration – Proven systems and processes
  • Dedicated Account Management – Single point of contact

Curated Experiences Portfolio

Signature Half-Day & Full-Day Experiences

Exclusively curated for B2B partners — bookable as standalone add-ons or woven into multi-day itineraries. Each experience is personally vetted, locally led, and fully customisable for groups of any size.

Culinary Romantic Adventure Cultural Wellness
Culinary · Half Day

Khmer Cooking Masterclass

Market tour at dawn, hands-on preparation of 4 signature Khmer dishes with a private chef, riverside dining setting. Perfect for incentive groups & cultural immersion.

3–4 hrs | Groups of 4–30
Romantic · Half Day

Sunset Gondola on Tonlé Sap

Private lacquer boat gliding across the great lake at golden hour. Champagne service, floating village passage, and a breathtaking Angkor horizon at dusk. Ideal for romantic add-ons.

2–3 hrs | 2–12 guests
Romantic · Evening

Private Dinner by Angkor

An exclusive candlelit dinner set within the temple grounds—Apsara dancers, torchlit pathways, and a five-course Khmer menu. Our most-requested romantic experience for honeymoon & anniversary packages.

3–4 hrs | 2–20 guests
Adventure · Half Day

Sunrise Horse Ride to the Temples

Guided horseback through rice paddies and jungle trails at dawn, arriving at a minor temple before the crowds. Suitable for all levels. A favourite for family and adventurous corporate groups.

3 hrs | 2–16 guests
Cultural · Half Day

Private Angkor Sunrise by Tuk-Tuk

A convoy of decorated tuk-tuks departing before dawn, your private historian-guide unlocking the Angkor complex before the public enters. Ends with a traditional Khmer breakfast in the forest.

4–5 hrs | 2–40 guests
Wellness · Half Day

Khmer Healing Ritual & Spa Journey

Traditional Khmer Kru Khmer massage, lemongrass herbal compress, and a guided meditation at a boutique wellness sanctuary. A restorative pairing for post-long-haul arrivals or incentive reward days.

2.5–3 hrs | Individual or group
