Da Nang

The Coastal Metropolis & Gateway to the Royal East

Where ancient marble peaks meet the timeless azure horizon of the East Sea

Framed by dramatic limestone crags, lush rainforest peninsulas, and sweeping crescent bays, Da Nang is Central Vietnam's modern crown jewel. Balancing sophisticated urban contemporary design with deep Cham heritage and French colonial nostalgia, the city serves as the ultimate coastal gateway for refined exploration.

From private helicopter transfers over Sun World Ba Na Hills and private access to sacred grotto sanctuaries within the Marble Mountains, to sunset yacht cruises along the Han River and ultra-luxury cliffside resorts in Son Tra, Da Nang redefines coastal Indochine elegance.

The Highlights & Experiences of Da Nang

Marble Mountains Sanctuary (Ngu Hanh Son)

Explore five sacred limestone peaks representing the five elements. Ascend through stone stairways into cavernous Buddhist shrines, subterranean grottoes, and elevated panoramic viewpoints overlooking the coastline.

Golden Bridge & Ba Na Hills Mountain Estate

Traverse the world's longest single-wire cable car to reach the iconic Golden Bridge, held aloft by giant stone hands 1,400 meters above sea level amidst mist-shrouded French colonial revival architecture.

Son Tra Nature Reserve & Lady Buddha

Journey into the coastal rainforest peninsula to behold the 67-meter monumental marble Lady Buddha at Linh Ung Pagoda, while observing rare endangered Red-shanked douc langurs in their natural habitat.

Dragon Bridge Evening River Cruise

Charter a luxury catamaran along the illuminated Han River. Witness the iconic steel Dragon Bridge emit spectacular displays of fire and water breathing on weekend evenings against the city skyline.

Museum of Cham Sculpture

Delve into the world's largest collection of Champa artifacts with a private expert curator. Discover exquisite 7th to 15th-century sandstone and terra cotta sculptures preserved within colonial arches.

My Khe Pristine Oceanfront Retreat

Unwind on continuous miles of powdery golden sand recognized globally for its pristine waters. Indulge in private beach cabana massages, artisan mixology, and world-class surfing instruction.

Hai Van Mountain Pass Vintage Drive

Embark on one of the world's most spectacular coastal mountain roads in a restored open-top vintage vehicle. Experience dramatic ocean vistas, French fortification ruins, and mountain air.

Non Nuoc Artisan Stone Masterclass

Visit a 300-year-old artisan village nestled at the base of the Marble Mountains. Meet master stone sculptors maintaining ancestral techniques of fine jade and marble carving.

Sun Wheel VIP Skyline Pavilion

Enjoy private VIP cabin access aboard one of the world's tallest observation wheels at 115 meters height, yielding breathtaking nighttime panoramas over illuminated bridges and ocean waters.

Cham Island Marine Reserve Expedition

Board a private speedboat to the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Cham Islands. Discover pristine coral reefs, private white-sand coves, and enjoy a secluded chef-prepared seafood lunch on the beach.

Han Market Chef-Guided Epicurean Tour

Navigate the vibrant aisles of Da Nang's historic 1940s trading hub with an expert chef. Sample Central Vietnamese specialties including Mi Quang noodles, Banh Xeo, and fresh exotic tropical fruits.

Son Tra Cliffside Haute Gastronomy

Experience world-class Michelin-caliber dining perched dramatically on the cliffs of Son Tra. Enjoy multi-course tasting menus paired with rare wines inside Bill Bensley-designed architectural wonders.
