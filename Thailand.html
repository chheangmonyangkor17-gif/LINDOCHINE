Thailand

"A kingdom of glittering spires and sapphire waters, where the fragrance of jasmine lingers in the air and the spirit of 'Sanuk' guides every moment."

In Thailand, tradition meets a modern soul in perfect harmony.

Travel Essentials

Capital Bangkok
Language Thai
Religion Buddhism
Currency Thai Baht (THB)

When to Journey

The Cool Season (Nov – Feb)

The most pleasant time for city exploration and island hopping. Expect sunny days and comfortable evenings.

The Green Season (June – Oct)

The landscape transforms into a lush paradise. Showers are brief, leaving the sights fresh and less crowded.
