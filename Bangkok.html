Bangkok

The Radiant City of Angels

Where ancient majesty meets modern grandeur

A journey through Bangkok reveals a captivating tapestry of contrasts—where golden spire reflections glimmer on the Chao Phraya River alongside glass-and-steel skyscrapers, intricate royal palaces, and vibrant canal-side communities.

Serving as the beating heart of Thailand, Krung Thep Maha Nakhon (Bangkok) merges centuries of royal heritage, world-class gastronomy, and sacred Buddhist traditions with a dynamic modern metropolis that never fails to enchant.

Curated Bangkok Experiences

Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

Private early-entry access with an expert historian to explore Thailand’s spiritual heart and revered Emerald Buddha within an opulent complex of gilded spires before public hours.

Wat Arun (Temple of Dawn)

Private morning or sunset viewing of the iconic porcelain-encrusted central spire standing majestically over the Chao Phraya River with sweeping city vistas.

Wat Pho & Reclining Buddha

Private guided tour of the 46-meter gold-leaf Reclining Buddha, followed by a traditional wellness session at the historical birthplace of Thai massage.

Jim Thompson House & Silk Heritage

Discover the exquisite teakwood sanctuary of the legendary American silk entrepreneur, featuring priceless Southeast Asian art collections and lush tropical gardens.

Talad Noi & Charoen Krung Walk

Expert-guided heritage trail through Bangkok's oldest riverside district, discovering historic Hokkien shrines, vintage artisan workshops, and restored shophouses.

Pruek Cruise Private Yacht Charter

A luxury three-story teak wood yacht charter offering multi-course Michelin-starred fine dining and champagne while gliding along the River of Kings.

Manohra Antique Rice Barge Voyage

An intimate candlelit cruise aboard a meticulously restored teak wood rice barge, serving authentic Thai royal gastronomy against lit river landmarks.

Thonburi Khlong & Big Buddha Cruise

Board a private longtail boat to navigate the canal stilt villages of Thonburi, visiting floating markets and the towering 69-meter Wat Paknam Great Buddha.

Michelin Chef’s Table Gastronomy

Bespoke tasting menus and wine pairings at celebrated epicurean institutions such as Potong, Sühring, Canvas, or ORE.

Chinatown VIP Street Food Discovery

A guided night discovery through Yaowarat's vibrant lanes, tasting legendary Michelin Bib Gourmand street vendor creations paired with private chauffeured transport.

High-Altitude Sky Venues

Elevated culinary and mixology experiences at sky venues such as Nobu Bangkok or Mahanakhon SkyBeach Club overlooking the glittering skyline.

Holistic Urban Wellness Retreats

Hydrotherapy circuits, IV vitamin infusions, and organic spa rituals at premier sanctuaries including Pañpuri Wellness, Aurasol, or The Siam Spa.

LV The Place & Visionary Journeys

An immersive cultural, exhibition, and culinary destination by Louis Vuitton at Gaysorn Amarin, featuring bespoke trunk displays and fine dining.

Embassy Diplomat VIP Cinema Suites

Ultra-exclusive cinema suites at Central Embassy featuring reclining cocoon beds, dedicated butler service, and private pre-show cocktail lounges.

Private Championship Golf Clubs

VIP tee-off privileges and personal caddie service at Bangkok’s most prestigious manicured private courses, complete with clubhouse dining.

Ayutthaya UNESCO Heritage Voyage

A private day voyage north via chauffeured transport or river cruiser to explore the monumental stone prangs and sacred monasteries of the ancient Siamese capital.
