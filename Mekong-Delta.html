Mekong Delta

The River of Nine Dragons & Waterway Sanctuary

Where ancient riverways weave through emerald palm groves and timeless floating communities

Fanning out across Southern Vietnam before meeting the East Sea, the Mekong Delta is a poetic tapestry of winding tributaries, lush mangrove swamps, and fertile tropical archipelagos. Life here unfolds to the rhythmic pulse of the tides, governed by an extraordinary harmony between nature and human tradition.

From dawn voyages aboard bespoke wooden sampans through bustling floating markets to private culinary tastings within century-old Indochine river estates, L'INDOCHINE unlocks the Delta's most secluded sanctuaries with quiet elegance and peerless craft.

The Highlights & Experiences of Mekong Delta

Cai Rang Private Sunrise Floating Market

Board a private wooden sampan at dawn to witness Can Tho's legendary floating bazaar, where local merchants trade vibrant harvests directly from wooden riverboats.

Ben Tre Emerald Coconut Canals

Traverse quiet palm-shaded canals via hand-rowed sampan, visiting organic coconut plantations, artisanal candy hearths, and secluded island estates.

Tra Su Cajuput Emerald Sanctuary

Glide in silent electric boats across carpeted green duckweed, exploring a sprawling 850-hectare avian reserve teeming with rare storks and waterbirds.

Cai Be Indochine River Estates

Step inside preserved 19th-century Mandarin mansions surrounding Cai Be, enjoying private multi-course luncheons served amidst antique courtyards.

Chau Doc Borderland & Sacred Temples

Explore the cultural melting pot near the Cambodian border, visiting sacred shrines along Sam Mountain and floating fish-farm villages.

Sa Dec Floral Fields & Colonial Legacy

Wander through vibrant flower villages and tour the historic Huynh Thuy Le ancient house, celebrated in French literary romance history.

Overnight Luxury Mekong River Charter

Charter a bespoke wooden vessel or boutique cruiser to sail between Saigon, Can Tho, and Phnom Penh in total privacy and modern comfort.

Đờn Ca Tài Tử & Epicurean Tasting

Savor traditional elephant-ear fish and coconut heart salads accompanied by private performances of UNESCO-recognized Southern folk music.

Vinh Long An Binh Island Cycle Path

Ride through car-free island paths shaded by rambutan and durian orchards, interacting with local artisans in tranquil riverine hamlets.

Tra Vinh Sacred Khmer Pagoda Heritage

Discover the unique cultural synthesis of Khmer Theravada Buddhism across Tra Vinh's 140 ancient, golden-roofed monastic complexes.

Thap Muoi Lotus Fields & Bird Reserve

Explore endless pink lotus fields in Dong Thap province, sampling fresh lotus tea and seasonal delicacies in wooden floating pavilions.

Secluded Delta Botanical Wellness Retreat

Rejuvenate with bespoke herbal steam baths and organic therapies infused with indigenous lemongrass, ginger, and Mekong lotus essences.
