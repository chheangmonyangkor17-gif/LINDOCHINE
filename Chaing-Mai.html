Chiang Mai

The Rose of the North

Where ancient Lanna heritage inspires tranquility

A journey through Chiang Mai reveals a serene tapestry of culture and nature—where golden chedis glisten against misty emerald mountain peaks, surrounded by historic moat walls, sacred forest temples, and tranquil riverbanks.

As the historic cultural heart of Northern Thailand, Chiang Mai seamlessly blends centuries of Lanna artistry, world-renowned Northern culinary traditions, and serene mountain sanctuaries with an artisanal, soulful luxury experience.

Curated Chiang Mai Experiences

Wat Phra That Doi Suthep

Private early-morning sunrise access with a private blessing ceremony by resident monks overlooking panoramic vistas of Chiang Mai valley from the mountain chedi.

Wat Chedi Luang & Old City

Expert-guided walk around the monumental 14th-century stone chedi, exploring historical Lanna architectural ruins embedded inside the ancient walled city.

Wat Umong Tunnel Temple

An immersive journey through 700-year-old subterranean tunnels beneath a forested sanctuary, followed by a private guided meditation session with a scholar monk.

Ethical Elephant Sanctuary Encounter

An exclusive non-riding ethical conservation encounter where guests walk alongside rescued Asian elephants through verdant jungle sanctuaries and river valleys.

Mae Ping River Sunset Voyage

Glide quietly down the idyllic Ping River on a traditional scorpion-tail boat or private luxury vessel, enjoying artisanal refreshments as twilight gathers.

Doi Inthanon Royal Pagodas Expedition

Ascend Thailand's highest peak to tour the twin Royal Stupas surrounded by misty cloud forest gardens, cascading waterfalls, and hilltribe settlements.

Organic Farm & Lanna Cooking Masterclass

Private garden harvesting and hands-on culinary workshop crafting signature northern dishes like Khao Soi under the guidance of a master chef.

Nimmanhaemin Design & Gallery Walk

A curated discovery of Chiang Mai's modern creative hub, visiting boutique craft studios, local contemporary art galleries, and specialty coffee houses.

Baan Tawai Artisan Craft Heritage

Exclusive access to private master woodcarvers and antique restoration ateliers in the famous handicraft village of Hang Dong.

Old City Night Market Discovery

Private VIP evening tour through atmospheric local night markets, tasting authentic Northern street gastronomy with private chauffeured escort.

Mae Tang Tea Estate & Tasting

Private excursion to terraced organic tea plantations in the northern hills, featuring artisanal tea tastings and a farm-to-table lunch overlooking the valleys.

Holistic Lanna Tok Sen Therapy

Experience ancient Tok Sen wooden hammer vibration therapy and warm herbal compress rituals at Northern Thailand’s premier wellness sanctuaries.

Wat Pha Lat Hidden Jungle Sanctuary

A tranquil trek along the Monk’s Trail to uncover a secluded stone temple hidden deep within the mountain forest by running waterfalls.

Sunrise Hot Air Balloon Flight

Float gracefully over mist-shrouded rice paddies and mountain peaks at dawn, followed by a champagne breakfast celebration upon landing.

Championship Mountain Golf Clubs

Exclusive tee times and personal caddie service at high-altitude 18-hole courses like Alpine Golf Resort Chiang Mai, framed by mountain vistas.
