Vietnam: A Luxury Nature & Adventure Discovery

The Experience

Vietnam Nature & Adventure

This bespoke 7-day journey is designed for the discerning traveler, offering an exclusive immersion into Vietnam's most spectacular natural landscapes and thrilling adventure experiences. Each detail, from private transfers to hand-picked accommodations, emphasizes unparalleled comfort, intimate service, and seamless exploration of the country's breathtaking natural wonders.

Duration7 Days / 6 Nights
ThemeNature & Adventure
Location Hanoi , Phong Nha, Ho chi Minh
CountryVietnam

Detailed Map Breakdown

Route: Hanoi → Phong Nha → Ho Chi Minh

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival Hanoi & Luxury Cruise Embarkation (Lan Ha Bay)

LOCATION Hanoi / Lan Ha Bay

ACTIVITIES Upon your arrival at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), a private, chauffeured luxury vehicle whisks you to Tuan Chau International Marina for a 2.5-hour journey. Board the exclusive Orchid Premium Cruise for your voyage into Lan Ha Bay. Settle into your private-balcony cabin before a gourmet welcome dinner as the cruise glides through dramatic limestone karsts. Enjoy bespoke service, fine wines, or a rejuvenating spa treatment.

ACCOMMODATION Orchid Premium Cruise

Dinner Gourmet Welcome: Meticulously Prepared Onboard Gastronomy

Day 2: Lan Ha Bay Private Exploration & Water Adventures

LOCATION Lan Ha Bay

ACTIVITIES Greet the sunrise with Tai Chi on the sundeck. Venture on a private kayaking excursion through emerald waters to discover secluded lagoons and caves. After a Vietnamese lunch, visit Trung Trang Cave or enjoy a bamboo boat ride through Ao Ech. Witness a spectacular sunset followed by an exquisite dinner and quiet stargazing from the deck.

ACCOMMODATION Orchid Premium Cruise

Lunch Authentic Flavors: Freshly Prepared Vietnamese Cruise Lunch

Day 3: Cruise Disembarkation & Journey to Phong Nha-Ke Bang

LOCATION Lan Ha Bay / Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final leisurely breakfast and Tai Chi session. Disembark and transfer to Hanoi (HAN) for a domestic flight to Dong Hoi Airport (VDH). A luxury private transfer will then transport you to Victory Road Villas in Phong Nha. Check into your private villa, designed for tranquility amidst the stunning natural landscape.

ACCOMMODATION Victory Road Villas

Dinner Welcome to the Wild: Gourmet Villa Dinner

Day 4: Phong Nha's Grand Caves Discovery

LOCATION Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES Start with a private guided tour of Paradise Cave (Thien Duong), marveling at its intricate stalactite formations. After lunch, head to Dark Cave (Hang Toi) for an exhilarating experience: zip-lining over the Chay River, mud-bathing in mineral-rich pools, and kayaking back along the river. Return to the villa for a relaxing evening.

ACCOMMODATION Victory Road Villas

Lunch Cave Explorer: Carefully Prepared Local Vietnamese Lunch

Day 5: Immersive Jungle Trek & River Cave Expedition

LOCATION Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES A day dedicated to immersive adventure tailored to your fitness. Join an expert-guided trek into the Tu Lan Cave System, Abandoned Valley, or Hang En Cave (3rd largest in the world). Experience jungle trekking, river crossings, and swimming into river caves led by reputable safety-focused operators.

ACCOMMODATION Victory Road Villas

Lunch Expedition Fuel: Gourmet Jungle Picnic Lunch

Day 6: Phong Nha Countryside & Cultural Immersion

LOCATION Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES Explore the countryside at a relaxed pace with a private cycling tour through rice paddies and villages. Enjoy leisure time at your villa's infinity pool in the afternoon. The journey concludes with a bespoke farewell dinner at the resort, celebrating your discoveries.

ACCOMMODATION Victory Road Villas

Lunch Bong Lai Valley: Fresh Farm-to-Table Lunch

Day 7: Departure from Dong Hoi

LOCATION Phong Nha / Departure

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final leisurely breakfast at your villa. A private luxury transfer will transport you to Dong Hoi International Airport (VDH) for your flight to Ho Chi Minh City for your onward international journey, concluding your "Nature & Adventure Discovery."

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Breakfast Departure Nourishment: Final Healthy Villa Breakfast
