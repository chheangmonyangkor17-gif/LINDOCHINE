20 Days / Cambodia, Vietnam, Laos, Thailand

Grand Indochina Journey: A Four-Country Escape

The Experience

Multi Country Wellness & Rejuvenation

20 Days / 19 Nights of Holistic healing, spiritual renewal, mindful experiences, tranquil luxury stays, and invigorating natural beauty.

Duration20 Days / 19 Nights
ThemeWellness & Rejuvenation
LocationSiem Reap, Ho chi Minh, Hoi An, Lang Co, Luang Prabang, Chaing Mai, Koh Samui
CountryCambodia, Vietnam, Laos, Thailand

Detailed Map Breakdown

Route: Siem Reap → Ho chi Minh → Hoi An → Lang Co → Luang Prabang → Chaing Mai → Koh Samui → Bangkok

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival in Siem Reap & Serene Welcome

LOCATION Siem Reap

ACTIVITIES Your wellness journey begins with a private luxury transfer upon arrival at Siem Reap Angkor International Airport (SAI). You'll be whisked away to Amansara, your serene and exclusive luxury retreat, formerly King Sihanouk's guesthouse, now a tranquil oasis perfectly situated for exploring Angkor. Settle into your minimalist-chic suite, designed for ultimate peace and privacy. Begin your rejuvenation with a personalized wellness consultation at Amansara's spa, followed by a bespoke welcome spa treatment designed to alleviate travel fatigue. Enjoy a light, healthy dinner at the resort, focusing on fresh, local ingredients, allowing for a peaceful start to your retreat.

ACCOMMODATION Amansara

Dinner Khmer Flavors: Farm-to-Table at The Dining Room

Day 2: Angkor's Spiritual Dawn & Ancient Reflection

LOCATION Siem Reap

ACTIVITIES Pre-dawn, embark on an unforgettable private sunrise experience at Angkor Wat, arranged to find a quiet viewing spot away from the main crowds. Witness the magnificent temple bathed in the soft glow of dawn, a truly spiritual moment. Enjoy a gourmet, healthy picnic breakfast specially prepared by Amansara near the temple grounds. Explore the awe-inspiring Angkor Thom, including the captivating faces of Bayon Temple and the serene Terrace of the Leper King, with your expert guide leading you through paths less traveled to maximize tranquility. Return to Amansara for a private yoga or meditation session tailored to your needs, perhaps by the serene pool. Conclude your day with a nourishing, plant-based dinner at the resort, designed for wellness and energy.

ACCOMMODATION Amansara

Breakfast Temple-Side Serenity: Gourmet Picnic at Angkor
Dinner Healing Cuisine: Plant-Based Khmer Tasting Menu

Day 3: Forest Temples & Mindful Immersion

LOCATION Siem Reap

ACTIVITIES This morning is dedicated to the atmospheric Ta Prohm, where ancient stone meets encroaching jungle, offering a powerful sense of nature's reclaiming power. Follow this with a visit to the intricately carved Banteay Srei Temple, known for its exquisite pink sandstone and delicate artistry, providing a more intimate temple experience. Enjoy a traditional Khmer massage at the resort, focusing on deep relaxation and muscle release. You might also partake in a private, gentle cooking class at Amansara, learning to prepare simple, healthy Khmer dishes using fresh, local ingredients. Dinner at the resort, or a quiet, local restaurant focusing on wholesome Cambodian cuisine.

ACCOMMODATION Amansara

Dinner Cultural Immersion: Traditional Khmer Dinner at the Village House

Day 4: Floating Villages & Rural Tranquility

LOCATION Siem Reap

ACTIVITIES Take a private boat trip on Tonle Sap Lake to visit a serene floating village, observing the unique rhythm of life on the water and gaining insight into local Cambodian culture away from the main tourist areas. This experience offers a quiet reflection on connection to nature and community. Return to the resort for leisure time, perhaps enjoying the pool or a quiet walk in the grounds. Engage in a private guided meditation session at Amansara, focusing on grounding and inner peace. Your evening will feature a special private dining experience, potentially a serene garden setting, with a customized healthy menu emphasizing local, organic produce.

ACCOMMODATION Amansara

Dinner Garden Serenity: Private Organic Farm-to-Table Experience

Day 5: Artisan Exploration & Journey to Vietnam

LOCATION Ho chi Minh

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a relaxed morning. You might choose to visit a local artisan workshop or a silk farm, observing traditional crafts and supporting local communities. This offers a gentle cultural immersion before your journey. Private transfer to Siem Reap Angkor International Airport for your luxury flight to Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Upon arrival at Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN), a private luxury transfer will take you to Park Hyatt Saigon, your sophisticated city hotel centrally located. After settling in, enjoy a light and healthy dinner at the hotel or a nearby restaurant known for its fresh, vibrant Vietnamese cuisine.

ACCOMMODATION Park Hyatt Saigon

Lunch Farewell Flavors: Wholesome Khmer Fare at Amansara
Dinner Vibrant Vietnam: Contemporary Fine Dining at Square One

Day 6: Ho Chi Minh City's Calm & Cultural Insight

LOCATION Ho Chi Minh

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a relaxed morning at Park Hyatt Saigon, perhaps with a refreshing swim or a light breakfast. You might opt for a gentle walking tour of the city's charming French colonial architecture, focusing on the beautiful Central Post Office and Notre Dame Cathedral (exterior). Engage in a private Vietnamese healthy cooking class, focusing on fresh herbs and light, vibrant flavors, then enjoy your creations for lunch. Spend the late afternoon at leisure, perhaps a gentle spa treatment at the hotel, designed to unwind from your journey. In the evening, enjoy a sophisticated, healthy dinner at Madame Lam - Vietnamese Contemporary Cuisine, a top restaurant known for its modern Vietnamese cuisine, reflecting on the day's cultural insights.

ACCOMMODATION Park Hyatt Saigon

Dinner Contemporary Soul: Modern Vietnamese at Madame Lam

Day 7: Flight to Hoi An & Riverside Serenity

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIESEnjoy a leisurely morning before a private transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) for your luxury domestic flight to Da Nang. Upon arrival at Da Nang International Airport (DAD), a private luxury transfer will take you to Anantara Hoi An Resort, nestled by the Thu Bon River, blending colonial charm with serene natural beauty. Settle into your beautiful riverside resort. Begin your Hoi An experience with a serene boat trip on the Thu Bon River at sunset, enjoying the peaceful views as the ancient town lights up. Follow this with a discovery of Hoi An Ancient Town, a UNESCO World Heritage site, by foot or gentle bicycle, admiring its lantern-lit streets and well-preserved architecture. Enjoy a delicious, light dinner at Hoi An Riverside Restaurant, savoring the tranquility of the illuminated town.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi an

Dinner Riverside Tranquility: Fresh Local Flavors at Hoi An Riverside

Day 8: Hoi An's Artisan Soul & Wellness Immersion

LOCATIONHoi An

ACTIVITIESBegin your day with a private outdoor yoga session by the river or at your resort, guided by a local instructor, focusing on breath and gentle movement. Afterwards, visit a local organic farm or herb garden, learning about traditional Vietnamese ingredients and their healing properties. Participate in a traditional Vietnamese healing massage or a bespoke spa treatment at your resort, designed for deep relaxation and rejuvenation. In the late afternoon, enjoy a tranquil tea ceremony, a mindful experience reflecting Vietnamese cultural traditions. Conclude your day with a fresh, farm-to-table dinner at a restaurant emphasizing local, seasonal produce.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Hoi An

Lunch Garden Harvest: Organic Farm-to-Table Lunch
Dinner Artisan Flavors: Modern Vietnamese at Art Space

Day 9: Coastal Escape & Lagoon Bliss

LOCATIONLang Co

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final leisurely morning in Hoi An. A private luxury transfer will take you on a scenic drive along the coast (potentially via the Hai Van Pass, offering breathtaking views) to Lang Co, home to Banyan Tree Lang Co. This secluded resort is nestled between mountains and a pristine beach, offering a perfect environment for deep relaxation. Settle into your private villa with a pool, designed for ultimate privacy and serenity. Dedicate your afternoon to unwinding at the resort's award-winning spa, indulging in personalized wellness therapies or a refreshing dip in your private plunge pool. Enjoy a healthy, gourmet dinner at one of the resort's exquisite restaurants, savoring the tranquil coastal ambiance.

ACCOMMODATION Bayan Tree Lang Co

Breakfast Riverside Farewell: Gourmet Artisan Breakfast in Hoi An
Dinner Saffron: Authentic Thai Flavors with a Coastal View

Day 10: Lagoon Wellness & Nature's Embrace

LOCATIONLang Co

ACTIVITIES Begin your day with a sunrise yoga session on the beach or by your villa, connecting with the peaceful energy of the coast. Enjoy a healthy breakfast featuring fresh fruits and local delights. Spend your morning at leisure, perhaps engaging in a wellness activity offered by the resort, such as a guided meditation walk, a session at the gym, or simply relaxing by the lagoon. Indulge in a holistic spa journey at Banyan Tree Spa, known for its Asian-inspired treatments and focus on mental and physical well-being. Consider a cooking demonstration focusing on healthy Vietnamese cuisine. Your evening will culminate with a private, romantic dinner by the beach or overlooking the lagoon, with a customized menu emphasizing fresh, nourishing ingredients.

ACCOMMODATION Bayan Tree Lang Co

Breakfast Colonial Morning: Indulgent Buffet at the Metropole
Breakfast Nourish & Restore: Healthy Superfood Breakfast at The Water Court
Dinner Destination Dining: Private Starlit Dinner on the Sands

Day 11: Flight to Luang Prabang & Spiritual Serenity

LOCATIONLuang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final leisurely morning in Lang Co. A private luxury transfer will take you to Da Nang International Airport (DAD) for your luxury flight to Luang Prabang, Laos. Upon arrival at Luang Prabang International Airport (LPQ), a private luxury transfer will whisk you to Amantaka, your tranquil luxury hotel, a former French colonial hospital transformed into an elegant sanctuary of peace and minimalist design, perfectly suited for contemplation. Settle into the serene ambiance of Amantaka. Begin your Luang Prabang wellness with a refreshing, light traditional Lao herbal welcome drink and a brief consultation with the resort's wellness specialist. Enjoy a soothing traditional Lao massage at the hotel's spa, designed to promote deep relaxation. Conclude your day with a light, nourishing dinner at your resort, embracing the quietude of Luang Prabang.

ACCOMMODATION Amantaka

Breakfast Coastal Departure: Final Gourmet Breakfast at Lang Co
Dinner Lao Mindful Dining: Light & Nourishing Fare at Amantaka

Day 12: Alms Giving, Temples & Mindful Mekong Views

LOCATIONLuang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Before dawn, participate respectfully in the Alms Giving Ceremony, observing the saffron-robed monks as they collect their daily offerings from locals. A private, respectful setup ensures an intimate and meaningful experience, fostering mindfulness. Return to the hotel for a healthy breakfast. Afterwards, explore the Royal Palace Museum, offering insight into Laos's royal history, and the exquisite Wat Xieng Thong, considered the most beautiful temple in Luang Prabang. Take a private, gentle boat trip along the Mekong River, appreciating the tranquil riverine life. Ascend Mount Phousi for panoramic sunset views over the town and the confluence of the Mekong and Nam Khan rivers, engaging in a mindful observation of the changing light. Conclude your day with a serene dinner at The Belle Rive Terrace, enjoying the peaceful atmosphere of Luang Prabang.

ACCOMMODATION Amantaka

Breakfast Spiritual Start: Traditional Lao Khao Piak & Tropical Fruits
Dinner Mekong Sunset: Riverside Dining at The Belle Rive Terrace

Day 13: Kuang Si Falls & Nature's Rejuvenation

LOCATIONLuang Prabang

ACTIVITIESJourney to the stunning multi-tiered Kuang Si Falls. Spend your morning immersed in this natural wonder, where you can swim in the invigorating turquoise pools, walk the forest paths, and visit the adjacent Bear Rescue Centre. This provides a refreshing connection with nature. Enjoy a healthy picnic lunch amidst the beauty of the falls. Return to Amantaka for leisure time, perhaps a period of silent meditation or journaling in your private suite. Engage in a private, gentle yoga session focused on restorative poses. For dinner, savor a bespoke plant-based menu at the resort, emphasizing fresh, local produce to further support your well-being.

ACCOMMODATION Amantaka

Lunch Wilderness Wellness: Organic Picnic at Kuang Si Falls
Dinner Garden Gastronomy: Bespoke Plant-Based Tasting Menu

Day 14: Rural Encounters & Traditional Healing

LOCATIONLuang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Take a private excursion to a local village in the Luang Prabang countryside, gaining insight into traditional Lao rural life and sustainable practices. This cultural immersion offers a grounding experience away from urban distractions. You might observe traditional weaving or rice farming. Experience a unique traditional Lao herbal sauna and scrub, known for its therapeutic properties and use of local herbs, followed by a relaxing tea ceremony. In the late afternoon, enjoy a contemplative stroll through the tranquil gardens of Amantaka. Your evening will include a private, serene dining experience with a focus on nourishing, wholesome Lao cuisine, allowing for a quiet conclusion to your day.

ACCOMMODATION Amantaka

Wellness Healing Infusion: Traditional Lao Herbal Tea Ceremony
Dinner Village Heritage: Wholesome & Nourishing Private Lao Dinner

Day 15: Leisure & Flight to Chiang Mai

LOCATIONChiang Mai

ACTIVITIESEnjoy a leisurely morning at Amantaka, perhaps with a final swim, a quiet meditation session, or a gentle walk around the resort grounds. This provides an opportunity for personal reflection and a relaxed transition. Private transfer to Luang Prabang International Airport for your luxury flight to Chiang Mai, Thailand. Upon arrival at Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX), a private luxury transfer will take you to Raya Heritage, your serene luxury resort nestled amidst lush landscapes by the Ping River. Settle into your tranquil haven. Enjoy a sophisticated, healthy welcome dinner at the resort, embracing the start of your Thai wellness experience.

ACCOMMODATION Raya Heritage

Breakfast Lao Farewell: Final Artisanal Breakfast at Amantaka
Dinner Khu Khao: Cross-Cultural Wellness Dining by the Ping River

Day 16: Ethical Elephant Connection & Sacred Blessings

LOCATIONChaing Mai

ACTIVITIESDedicate your morning to an exclusive, ethical elephant sanctuary, where you'll enjoy a private, unhurried experience interacting respectfully with rescued elephants. Learn about their care, feed them, and observe them in their natural habitat, fostering a deep connection with these magnificent creatures. Visit the revered Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, perched on a mountain overlooking the city. Arrange a private blessing ceremony with monks for spiritual reflection and good fortune for your journey together, a profoundly peaceful and traditional experience. Conclude your day by indulging in a hands-on Thai cooking class at Grandma's Home Cooking School, focusing on healthy, aromatic Thai dishes, which you will then enjoy for dinner.

ACCOMMODATION Raya Heritage

Breakfast Sustainable Start: Organic Garden-to-Table Breakfast at Raya
Dinner Grandma's Kitchen: Healthy Organic Thai Cooking Class

Day 17: Cultural Wellness & Flight to Koh Samui

LOCATIONKoh Samui

ACTIVITIES Begin your day with a traditional Thai yoga (Ruesi Datton) session, a gentle practice focusing on flexibility and energy flow. Enjoy a wholesome breakfast. Explore Chiang Mai's vibrant artisan scene with a private visit to exclusive artisan workshops (e.g., silk weaving, ceramics), observing traditional craftsmanship and mindful creation. Enjoy a final healthy lunch in Chiang Mai. Private transfer to Chiang Mai International Airport for your luxury domestic flight to Koh Samui. Upon arrival at Samui International Airport (USM), a private luxury transfer will take you to Kamalaya Koh Samui, a world-renowned holistic wellness sanctuary nestled on a hillside overlooking the Gulf of Thailand. Settle into your serene accommodation and begin your personalized wellness program. Enjoy a light, detox-friendly dinner as part of your Kamalaya experience.

ACCOMMODATION Kamalaya Koh Samui

Lunch Lanna Vitality: Final Healthy Thai Lunch in Chiang Mai
Dinner Detox Gastronomy: Healing & Wholesome Dinner at Soma

Day 18: Holistic Rejuvenation at Kamalaya

LOCATIONKoh Samui

ACTIVITIES Dedicate your entire day to personalized wellness at Kamalaya Koh Samui. Your program will be tailored to your goals, potentially including: Guided meditation, group yoga or Pilates class; Personalized spa treatment (e.g., Traditional Thai massage, detoxifying wrap, Ayurvedic therapy); Holistic consultation (naturopathy, traditional Chinese medicine), fitness session, or relaxation by the pool. Enjoy a healthy, gourmet wellness dinner, designed to nourish and cleanse, as part of Kamalaya's integrated approach to well-being. Participate in an evening talk or workshop on a wellness topic (e.g., nutrition, mindfulness).

ACCOMMODATION Kamalaya Koh Samui

Breakfast Vitality Start: Nutritive Buffet & Superfood Elixirs at Soma
Dinner Healing Flavors: Gourmet Detox Cuisine Overlooking the Gulf

Day 19: Deep Healing & Coastal Tranquility

LOCATIONKoh Samui

ACTIVITIES Continue your personalized wellness journey at Kamalaya Koh Samui. Your day could include: Specialized healing session (e.g., reiki, chakra balancing, lymphatic drainage massage); Private yoga or meditation session, or participation in a complimentary activity like a cooking demonstration of healthy cuisine; Time for reflection, journaling, enjoying the serene natural surroundings, or another tailored treatment. Enjoy your final wellness dinner at Kamalaya, perhaps with a special farewell healthy mocktail. Take a quiet evening stroll along the beach or participate in a serene evening ritual.

ACCOMMODATION Kamalaya Koh Samui

Breakfast Radiant Awakening: Superfood Buffet & Fresh Tropical Fruits at Soma
Dinner Coastal Farewell: Final Wellness Degustation & Signature Mocktails

Day 20: Departure from Koh Samui

LOCATIONKoh Samui

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final healthy breakfast at Kamalaya, perhaps with a last meditation session or a gentle walk to absorb the tranquil energy. Time at leisure to relax, pack, and prepare for your departure, feeling fully rejuvenated. Private luxury transfer to Samui International Airport (USM) for your onward international departure to Bangkok International Airport (BKK), carrying with you a renewed sense of well-being and cherished memories of your transformative journey.

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Breakfast Nourished Departure: Final Wholesome Breakfast at Soma
Lunch Island Fare: Light & Healthy Lakeside Lunch at Amrita
