7 Days / Vietnam

Vietnam: A Luxury Cultural & Historical Immersion

The Experience

Vietnam Culture & Heritage

This meticulously crafted 7-day journey offers an unparalleled luxury immersion into Vietnam's rich cultural tapestry and profound historical narratives. Designed for discerning travelers, this itinerary focuses on the ancient capital of Hanoi, the imperial city of Hue, and the enchanting ancient town of Hoi An, ensuring private access, expert guidance, and opulent accommodations that blend heritage charm with modern comfort.

Duration7 Days / 6 Nights
ThemeCultural & Heritage
Location Hanoi , Hue , Hoi an
CountryVietnam

Detailed Map Breakdown

Route: Hanoi → Hue → Hoi An

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival & French Quarter Grandeur in Hanoi

LOCATION Hanoi

ACTIVITIES Upon your morning or afternoon arrival at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), a private luxury transfer will whisk you to the iconic Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi. Settle into your exquisitely appointed room or suite, a sanctuary of colonial grandeur. In the late afternoon, begin your cultural immersion with a leisurely introductory stroll through Hanoi's elegant French Quarter. Admire the beautiful colonial architecture, explore charming tree-lined boulevards, and visit the majestic St. Joseph's Cathedral. In the evening, enjoy a sophisticated welcome dinner at the Metropole's own Le Beaulieu restaurant, savoring exquisite French-Vietnamese fusion cuisine.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi

Dinner Welcome Dinner: French-Vietnamese Fusion at Le Beaulieu

Day 2: Ancient Capital's Heart & Soul

LOCATION Hanoi

ACTIVITIES Begin with a private guided tour of Hanoi's historical landmarks, including the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Complex (exterior) and the serene Temple of Literature. Savor an authentic Vietnamese lunch at a local fine-dining restaurant known for traditional specialties. In the afternoon, navigate the vibrant Old Quarter with your expert guide, discovering hidden temples and merchant houses. In the evening, experience the traditional Water Puppet Show, a centuries-old art form, followed by a delightful dinner specializing in rich Hanoi heritage cuisine.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi

Lunch Authentic Northern Specialties at a Fine-Dining Local Venue

Day 3: Cultural Connections & Hands-on Fun

LOCATION Hanoi

ACTIVITIES Continue your historical journey at the Imperial Citadel of Thang Long, a UNESCO site serving as Vietnam's political center for a millennium. After lunch in the Old Quarter, visit the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology for a deeper understanding of the country's diverse ethnic groups. As evening falls, enjoy a memorable farewell dinner at The Rhythms Restaurant, offering exquisite Vietnamese fusion cuisine and panoramic views of Hoan Kiem Lake.

ACCOMMODATION Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi

Dinner Farewell Dinner: Panoramic City Views at The Rhythms

Day 4: Scenic Train Journey to Imperial Hue

LOCATION Hue

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final Metropole breakfast before a private transfer to the station. Board a first-class train cabin for a scenic journey south, witnessing the changing landscapes of rural Vietnam. Upon arrival in Hue, transfer to the elegant Azerai La Residence. Settle into this colonial masterpiece and enjoy a relaxed dinner at Le Parfum, featuring fine Vietnamese and international cuisine overlooking the Perfume River.

ACCOMMODATION Azerai La Residence

Dinner Riverside Dining at Le Parfum: Vietnamese & International Cuisine

Day 5: Emperors' Legacies & Perfume River Serenity

LOCATION Hue

ACTIVITIES Explore the grand courtyards and ancient temples of the Imperial Citadel. Following an imperial cuisine lunch, embark on a private dragon boat cruise along the Perfume River to visit the iconic Thien Mu Pagoda and the magnificent Royal Tombs. In the evening, experience a "Royal Dinner" in a restored garden house, featuring traditional dishes accompanied by exquisite imperial music performances.

ACCOMMODATION Azerai La Residence

Dinner Immersive Royal Garden House Dinner with Traditional Music

Day 6: Coastal Drive to Ancient Hoi An

LOCATION Hoi An

ACTIVITIES Depart Hue for a private transfer to Hoi An via the breathtaking Hai Van Pass, enjoying panoramic coastal views. Check into the luxurious Hotel Royal Hoi An - MGallery. In the afternoon, walk through the UNESCO-listed Ancient Town to see the Japanese Covered Bridge and historic merchant houses. At night, dine at a romantic riverside restaurant as hundreds of colorful lanterns illuminate the town.

ACCOMMODATION Hotel Royal Hoi An - MGallery

Dinner Riverside Culinary Art: Flavors of Hoi An at Mango Mango

Day 7: Crafts, Cuisine & Farewell from Vietnam

LOCATION Hoi An / Departure

ACTIVITIES Immerse yourselves in a refined morning cooking class, learning the secrets of Hoi An's culinary heritage and enjoying your creations for lunch. Spend your final afternoon at leisure for boutique shopping or visiting master tailors like Yaly Couture. A private luxury transfer will then take you to Da Nang International Airport (DAD) for your onward journey, carrying cherished memories of Vietnam.

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Lunch Culinary Masterclass: Hand-prepared Hoi An Delicacies
