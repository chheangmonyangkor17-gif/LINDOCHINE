Anantara Angkor Resort, Khmer Villa Grandeur

Anantara Angkor Resort captures the magnificent essence of a grand Khmer villa, serving as an upscale boutique sanctuary for sophisticated modern explorers tracing the nearby ancient wonders of Angkor Wat. Conceived with deep architectural references to traditional royal heritage, the property emphasizes symmetrical columns, rich indigenous woodwork, and beautifully landscaped garden expanses. Positioned flawlessly within easy reach of both the vibrant heart of Siem Reap and the archaeological park checkpoints, the resort frames an intimate pool courtyard inspired by ancient temple pools. Across its 39 luxurious suites, layouts are characterized by highly intricate local textiles, handcrafted details, and sweeping private balconies or terraces. Elite tier configurations feature massive plunge-pool courtyards and dedicated butler provisions to maximize personalized comfort. Dining celebrations present a sensory crossing, where the refined indoor settings and breezy open pavilions serve sophisticated interpretations of ancient Khmer royalty recipes alongside premium global fare, accompanied by a world-class Anantara Spa focused on healing lotus-infused therapies and modern wellness restoration.

Style

Authentic Khmer villa design enriched with heritage woodwork, artisanal sandstone carvings, and contemporary boutique luxury details.

Situation

Conveniently situated in Siem Reap, offering seamless connectivity to both downtown street markets and the temple entry gates.

Capacity

39 beautifully appointed, spacious luxury suites configured around a central verdant courtyard swimming pool.

The Most

An outstanding tradition of genuine, warm hospitality coupled with uniquely structured cultural engagement programs and resort butler care.

The Experiments

  • Traverse majestic temple ruins with customized itineraries organized by the resort's private Experience Gurus
  • Master traditional Cambodian gastronomy through hands-on Spice Spoons culinary masterclasses
  • Receive a meaningful spiritual blessing from local elders at historic neighborhood pagodas
  • Uncover scenic cycling paths and vibrant rural life on curated countryside village excursions
  • Rejuvenate mind and body with sunrise yoga, holistic therapies, and Khmer massage at the signature spa

The Extraordinary

For the ultimate romantic celebration, enjoy Anantara's tailored 'Dining by Design' experience. Share an elegant multi-course custom meal in a secluded garden corner or under a canopy of tropical flowers, complete with a private chef, dedicated table butler service, and soft traditional string melodies underneath the Siem Reap evening stars.

Ethics

Anantara Angkor Resort remains committed to community empowerment by actively sourcing raw decor elements from local weaving cooperatives. The resort supports local neighborhood orphanages, funds essential healthcare drives, and promotes eco-friendly plastic elimination strategies to protect the fragile heritage ecosystems of Siem Reap.
