Travel Essentials

Capital Phnom Penh
Language Khmer
Religion Theravada Buddhism
Currency USD & KHR

*USD is used for most transactions; Riel for small change.

When to Journey

The Golden Season (November – April)

The most popular time to visit. Expect clear blue skies and mild temperatures ideal for exploring temple ruins. From February onwards, the heat begins to intensify, offering a true tropical experience.

The Emerald Season (May – October)

The monsoon rains bring the countryside to life in vibrant shades of green. While brief daily showers are common, the moats of Angkor are full, the dust is settled, and the sites are much more peaceful.

Curator's Note: The months of November through January offer the most comfortable climate for extensive trekking.
