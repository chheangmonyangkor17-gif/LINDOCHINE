The Experience

A golden-hour retreat on the historic waters of the Khmer empire

The Sunset Gondola Cruise on the protective moat of Angkor Thom is carefully designed for high-end travelers who value absolute peace, spatial isolation, and historical poetry. As the bulk of temple visitors return to their hotels, your clients cross the stone threshold onto a hand-carved, traditional Khmer rowing boat—vividly reminiscent of the royal watercraft pictured on the walls of Bayon.

The boat glides along the shadowed stone walls of the final great capital city of the Khmer Empire. With only the rhythmic splash of the oarsman's blade breaking the silence, the journey paths past hidden historical bastions and old canopy banks, offering unique, low-angle vistas of Prasat Chrung temple. As light drops, cold drinks are served alongside artisanal bites directly on deck.

Built purposefully as a signature luxury add-on for regional premium agencies, this itinerary flows perfectly directly following a busy afternoon of monument walking, operating as a rewarding, slow-paced transition into the evening hours.

  • Private chartered traditional Khmer rowing boat manned by a dedicated local boatman
  • Peaceful navigation along the secluded southwest section of the Angkor Thom city moat
  • Low-impact stopover to view sunset from the ancient stone walls of Prasat Chrung temple
  • Premium onboard refreshment service including tailored snacks and cold selection wines
  • Idyllic lighting windows curated for exclusive, private portrait photography on the water
  • White-label operations effortlessly branded under your distinct agency criteria

The Programme

As the sun sets, hour by hour

The cruise is timed strictly to match solar patterns. Departures vary by 15 minutes depending on seasonal shifts between wet and dry conditions, ensuring optimized positioning for your client's twilight viewing window.

1
16:30 · South Gate Arrival

Embarkation at the Ancient Gate

Your clients are collected from their luxury resort lobby and driven directly to the monumental South Gate of Angkor Thom. After passing the line of stone gods and demons, guests bypass the walking trails and step down to a private boarding jetty on the serene moat waters.

2
16:45 · Moat Traversal

The Silent Moat Drift

The gondola sets off, staying close to the base of the massive earth and laterite city walls. The boatman relies purely on classic hand paddles, ensuring an incredibly quiet ride. The setting sun reflects off the giant lily pads, and the calls of hidden forest birds provide a natural soundtrack.

3
17:15 · Refreshment Service

Canapés & Twilight Toasts

As the sky adopts pink and orange tones, the hostess sets up an onboard table with fine linen. Premium chilled white wine, local craft beers, or mineral waters are un-corked and accompanied by a selection of delicate Cambodian-inspired finger hors d'oeuvres.

4
17:40 · Temple Bastion Stop

Prasat Chrung Sunken View

The boat drifts right to the corner ridge of the old fortifications below Prasat Chrung. Guests enjoy a clear perspective of this small sandstone monument perched above the corner walls. It is an unmatched vantage point to toast the absolute end of daylight over the western horizons.

5
18:30 · Disembarkation

Return Jetty & Transfer Home

The vessel arrives gently at the final landing point under the early stars. Guests step ashore directly into their waiting private car for a comfortable air-conditioned transfer back to their resort or directly to their fine-dining dinner venue in Siem Reap town.

Good to Know

Practical Information

Park Regulations

Every guest must hold a valid Angkor Archaeological Park admission pass to cross the checkpoints into the South Gate zone, even if not entering temple gates.

Weather Alternatives

In case of surprise heavy afternoon downpours, cruises can be held for 30 minutes or safely rescheduled to the following afternoon subject to boat availability.

What to Wear

Comfortable smart-casual resort wear. Footwear should be easy to slip off for boarding. A light wrap or scarf is recommended for twilight breezes.

Booking Windows

Operations demand a 24-hour minimum safety notification. Cancellations made inside 24 hours draw full cancellation costs due to catering lock-ins.
