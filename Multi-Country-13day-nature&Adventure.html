13 Days / Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, Cambodia

The Grand Indochina Adventure

The Experience

Multi Country Adventure Journeys

This itinerary 13 Days is a seamless blend of high-end comfort and exhilarating adventure, designed for a traveler who seeks both the thrill of the wild and the tranquility of world-class service of Indochina

Duration13 Days / 12 Nights
ThemeNature & Adventure Journeys
LocationHa Noi, Ha long Bay, Phong Nha, Chaing Mai,Luang Prabang, Siem Reap
Country Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, Cambodia

Detailed Map Breakdown

Route: Ha Noi → Ha Long Bay → Phong Nha → Chaing Mai → Luang Prabang → Siem Reap

Detailed Itinerary Breakdown

Day 1: Arrival in Hanoi & Gastronomic Discovery

LOCATION Hanoi

ACTIVITIES Upon your arrival at Noi Bai International Airport (HAN), a private luxury transfer will take you to the opulent, opera-themed Capella Hanoi. After settling into your room, a private, guided walking tour will lead you through the historic streets of Hanoi's Old Quarter, where the air is alive with the sounds and smells of street life and the unique French colonial architecture provides a beautiful backdrop. Your culinary journey will begin with a magnificent dinner at Gia Restaurant, a Michelin-starred establishment renowned for its masterful reinterpretation of traditional Vietnamese cuisine with a stunning seasonal tasting menu.

ACCOMMODATION Capella Hanoi

Dinner Contemporary Heritage: Vietnamese Tasting Menu at Gia

Day 2: The Mystical Beauty of Ha Long Bay

LOCATION Ha Long Bay

ACTIVITIESFollowing a gourmet breakfast, a private car will transport you to Ha Long Bay. You will board the Orchid Classic Cruise, a boutique luxury junk boat, and be welcomed with a refreshing drink and an introduction to your journey. Enjoy a multi-course lunch as you sail through the magnificent limestone karsts. The highlight of the afternoon is sea kayaking to a hidden lagoon and a secluded beach, followed by an onboard cooking class to learn the art of preparing Vietnamese spring rolls. As the sun sets, a romantic dinner featuring fresh seafood and fine wine will be served on the deck of your boat under a canopy of stars.

ACCOMMODATION Orchid Premium Cruise

Breakfast Gourmet Starters: Artisan Breakfast at Capella Hanoi
Dinner Bay Splendor: Fresh Seafood & Fine Wine under the Stars

Day 3: The Caving Expedition of Phong Nha

LOCATION Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES After a final breakfast on the boat, you will disembark and take a private transfer to Hanoi airport for a short flight to Dong Hoi (VDH). A private car will then take you to Chày Lập Farmstay, a luxurious and eco-focused retreat. After a light lunch, you will embark on a guided tour of the stunning Paradise Cave, a cavern of immense scale and breathtaking formations. Your day will conclude with a delicious, farm-to-table dinner at the farmstay's restaurant, which specializes in fresh, local ingredients from the surrounding countryside.

ACCOMMODATION Chay Lap Farmstay

Dinner Harvest to Hearth: Farm-to-Table Vietnamese Dining

Day 4: Expedition to Tu Lan Cave System

LOCATION Phong Nha

ACTIVITIES Following a hearty breakfast, you'll meet with your expert guides from Oxalis Adventure for a full-day expedition into the Tu Lan Cave System, beginning with a trek through the jungle. The adventure continues as you swim into the impressive caves, navigating their magnificent interiors and subterranean rivers. A gourmet picnic lunch is provided during your trek. You will then return to the comfort of your farmstay for a well-deserved, hearty dinner to celebrate the day's accomplishments.

ACCOMMODATION Chay Lap Farmstay

Lunch Wilderness Fuel: Gourmet Jungle Picnic by Oxalis
Dinner Explorer’s Feast: Hearty Post-Trek Dinner at Chày Lập

Day 5: Arrival & Mekong Sunset in Luang Prabang

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES Upon arrival at Luang Prabang (LPQ) via a Flight from Dong Hoi (VDH), you will be taken to the magnificent Rosewood Luang Prabang, a serene retreat nestled in a forested landscape. After settling in and enjoying the resort's tranquil surroundings, you will be driven to the base of Mount Phousi in the late afternoon. You'll climb to the summit for a truly breathtaking, panoramic view of the sun setting over the Mekong and Nam Khan rivers, bathing the city in a golden light. Following the sunset, you will be transferred to Manda de Laos, a fine-dining establishment known for its traditional Laotian cuisine and exquisite ambiance amidst UNESCO-classified lily ponds, for a memorable dinner.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Welcome Riverside Refresh: Afternoon Tea at Rosewood Luang Prabang
Dinner Lao Heritage: Fine Dining Amidst the Lily Ponds at Manda de Laos

Day 6: Kuang Si Waterfalls & Village Trek

LOCATION Luang Prabang

ACTIVITIES After a leisurely breakfast, a private car will take you to the spectacular Kuang Si Waterfalls. You'll have ample time to hike to the top of the falls and swim in the beautiful turquoise pools. On the way back to the resort, your guide will take you to a local village of the Khmu or Hmong ethnic minorities for a deeper cultural immersion. Your dinner this evening will be a refined experience at the Rosewood's Great House, which offers a delicious menu of Lao and Western dishes in a beautiful, open-air setting.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Dinner Estate Dining: Lao & Western Fusion at The Great House

Day 7: Kayaking on the Mekong River

LOCATIONLuang Prabang

ACTIVITIESYou'll embark on a private kayaking tour down a serene stretch of the Mekong River, paddling past remote villages and lush scenery. In the afternoon, you will stop to visit the mystical Pak Ou Caves, which are filled with thousands of Buddha statues, and enjoy a gourmet picnic lunch by the river. Your evening will conclude with a memorable private dinner experience arranged by the Rosewood, perhaps at a secluded spot on the property, complete with a flowing waterfall.

ACCOMMODATION Rosewood Luang Prabang

Lunch Riverside Serenity: Gourmet Picnic by the Pak Ou Caves
Dinner Enchanted Evening: Private Waterfall Dining Experience

Day 8: Arrival & Thai Cooking in Chiang Mai

LOCATIONChaing Mai

ACTIVITIESUpon your direct flight from Luang Prabang (LPQ) to Chiang Mai (CNX), a private transfer will take you to the elegant Anantara Chiang Mai Resort. After settling in, you will enjoy a private Thai cooking class with the resort's chef, where you will learn to create authentic Northern Thai dishes. For dinner, you can enjoy the delicious dishes you prepared during your class, or head to The Gad Lanna for an exquisite fine-dining experience at a renowned Northern Thai restaurant.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Chaing Mai Resort

Lunch Lanna Flavors: Authentic Northern Thai Market Lunch
Dinner Chef's Table: Your Hand-Crafted Northern Thai Creations

Day 9: Ethical Elephant Sanctuary

LOCATIONChaing Mai

ACTIVITIES A private guide will take you to a reputable ethical elephant sanctuary, such as Elephant Nature Park, where you will spend the day learning about these gentle giants and their conservation. The experience continues in the afternoon as you help prepare their food and walk with them, with a delicious vegetarian buffet lunch served at the sanctuary. The evening is then free for you to explore Chiang Mai's vibrant night market or to relax and enjoy a romantic dinner at your resort.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Chaing Mai Resort

Breakfast Riverside Sunrise: Signature Breakfast at Anantara
Lunch Sanctuary Feast: Traditional Vegetarian Buffet
Dinner Night Market Delights or Riverside Dining at Anantara

Day 10: Lagoon Wellness & Nature's Embrace

LOCATIONChaing Mai

ACTIVITIES Your private guide will lead a trekking tour in Doi Inthanon National Park, Thailand's highest peak, with a hike that takes you through stunning cloud forests and past waterfalls. In the afternoon, you will enjoy a gourmet picnic lunch amidst the park's natural beauty and visit the magnificent twin pagodas. Your day will conclude with a farewell dinner at Paak Dang, a highly-rated riverside restaurant known for its exquisite Thai dishes and peaceful ambiance.

ACCOMMODATION Anantara Chaing Mai Resort

Breakfast Riverside Morning: Signature Buffet at Anantara Chiang Mai
Lunch Cloud Forest Picnic: Gourmet Al Fresco Lunch at Doi Inthanon
Dinner Thai Farewell: Riverside Dining at Paak Dang

Day 11: Arrival & Tranquil Retreat

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIES Upon your flight from Chiang Mai (CNX) to Siem Reap (SAI), a private driver will take you to the breathtaking Zannier Hotels Phum Baitang, a resort designed to resemble a traditional Cambodian village. After settling in and enjoying the peaceful surroundings, you will dine at the resort's high-end Khmer restaurant, Phum Baitang Restaurant, for an exquisite introduction to contemporary Cambodian cuisine.

ACCOMMODATION Zannier Hotel Phum Baitang

Breakfast Farewell Chiang Mai: Riverside Breakfast at Anantara
Dinner Bay Phum: Fine Dining Khmer Flavors Amidst Rice Paddies

Day 12: Angkor Temple Cycling Adventure

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIES A guided private mountain biking tour will take you to explore the temples of Angkor Wat and Angkor Thom. You'll cycle on quiet jungle trails between the temples, seeing less-visited ruins, and enjoy a delicious packed lunch in a serene spot. For a final, memorable evening, you will dine at Embassy Restaurant, which is celebrated for its creative, fine-dining take on traditional Khmer gastronomy.

Day 13: Departure from Siem Reap

LOCATIONSiem Reap

ACTIVITIES Enjoy a final breakfast amidst the tranquil surroundings of your resort. A private transfer will then take you to Siem Reap Angkor International Airport (SAI) for your onward flight, concluding your grand adventure.

ACCOMMODATION N/A (Departure)

Breakfast A Khmer Farewell: Final Village Breakfast at Phum Baitang
