Privacy
Policy

Last Updated — January 2026

At L'Indochine, the privacy of our guests and partners is held with the same reverence as the journeys we create. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and respect your personal information when you engage with us — online, by correspondence, or through the planning and delivery of your journey.

01 — Who We Are

Data Controller

L'Indochine is the data controller responsible for your personal information. We are a luxury Destination Management Company headquartered in Siem Reap, Kingdom of Cambodia, operating journeys across Southeast Asia.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our team at:

  • Email: privacy@lindochine.com
  • Phone: +855 95 267 257
  • Address: Siem Reap, Kingdom of Cambodia
02 — Information We Collect

What Personal Data We Hold

We collect personal information only when it is necessary to provide our services or to communicate with you. The categories of data we may collect include:

Category Examples Why We Collect It
Identity Data Full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number Journey bookings, visa assistance, hotel check-in requirements
Contact Data Email address, phone number, postal address Correspondence, booking confirmations, emergency contact
Journey Preferences Dietary requirements, accessibility needs, room preferences, special occasions Delivering a personalised, bespoke experience
Health Data Medical conditions relevant to travel, allergies Safety and wellbeing during your journey (collected only with consent)
Financial Data Payment information (processed securely by our payment providers) Processing deposits and balances
Technical Data IP address, browser type, pages visited, cookies Website performance, analytics, user experience improvement
Communications Data Emails, enquiry forms, call notes Responding to enquiries and maintaining service records

We do not collect data we do not need. We will never ask for sensitive information such as racial origin, political opinions, or religious beliefs unless directly relevant to a specific journey service and only with your explicit consent.

03 — How We Collect Your Data

Sources of Information

We collect personal information through the following channels:

  • Directly from you — when you submit an enquiry form, email us, call us, or complete a booking.
  • Travel agents and B2B partners — who share your information on your behalf to arrange your journey with us.
  • Our website — through cookies, contact forms, and the journey planning tool.
  • Third-party services — such as accommodation providers, airlines, and activity operators who may share relevant guest information with us as part of journey delivery.
04 — How We Use Your Data

Purposes & Legal Basis

We process your personal data only for specific, legitimate purposes. Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Performance of a contract — processing your booking, arranging transport, accommodation, guides, and all journey components.
  • Legal obligation — complying with immigration, customs, and financial record-keeping requirements.
  • Legitimate interests — improving our services, communicating with past guests about new offerings, and protecting our business from fraud.
  • Consent — sending you marketing communications or processing special-category data such as health or dietary information.

"We will never use your personal data for purposes incompatible with why you shared it with us. Your trust is the foundation of every journey we craft."

05 — Sharing Your Data

With Whom We Share Information

To deliver your journey, we share only the data strictly necessary with the following categories of third parties:

  • Accommodation providers — hotels, resorts, and boutique lodges included in your itinerary.
  • Transport operators — airlines, private drivers, charter services, and river cruise operators.
  • Local guides and activity operators — vetted specialists who form part of your journey experience.
  • Payment processors — secure third-party platforms for processing deposits and balances.
  • Travel agents and B2B partners — where your booking was made through an intermediary.
  • Government and immigration authorities — where required by law for entry or border requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for their own marketing purposes. All supplier partners are required to maintain appropriate data security standards and to use your information only for the purposes of delivering your journey.

06 — International Data Transfers

Cross-Border Data Flows

As a Southeast Asia travel specialist, the nature of our business means your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries including Cambodia, Vietnam, Thailand, Laos, and Myanmar — and potentially beyond, depending on your journey.

These countries may have different data protection standards than your home country. We take appropriate contractual and organisational safeguards to ensure your data receives an equivalent level of protection wherever it is processed.

For guests based in the European Union or United Kingdom, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions where applicable to legitimise such transfers.

07 — Data Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, and reporting requirements:

  • Active booking and journey data — retained for 7 years from the date of travel to comply with financial and legal obligations.
  • Enquiry data (no booking made) — retained for 12 months, after which it is securely deleted.
  • Marketing preferences — retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Website analytics and technical data — retained for up to 24 months in aggregated, anonymised form.

Upon expiry of the relevant retention period, your data is securely deleted or anonymised so it can no longer be linked to you.

08 — Cookies & Website Tracking

How We Use Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our content. The types of cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance cookies — collect anonymised analytics data (e.g. Google Analytics) to help us improve page performance and content.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences such as language or region settings.
  • Marketing cookies — used to deliver relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness. These are only placed with your consent.

You may manage your cookie preferences at any time via your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. Disabling non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use our website, though some personalisation features may be limited.

09 — Data Security

How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your personal data seriously and have implemented appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration. These include:

  • Secure, encrypted transmission of data via HTTPS across our website and communications.
  • Access controls ensuring only authorised personnel can access personal data relevant to their role.
  • Secure, password-protected systems and regular security reviews.
  • Staff training on data protection obligations and best practices.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authorities as required by applicable law.

10 — Your Rights

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data where there is no legitimate reason for us to continue processing it.
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to suspend processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@lindochine.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

11 — Marketing Communications

Staying in Touch

If you have booked with us or made an enquiry, we may, with your consent, send you occasional communications about new journeys, seasonal destinations, and curated travel inspiration relevant to your interests.

You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly. Withdrawing consent for marketing will not affect the delivery of your booked journey or any transactional communications.

We will never send unsolicited marketing without your prior consent, and we will never share your details with third-party marketers.

12 — Children's Privacy

Protecting Younger Travellers

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. When a child is part of a family booking, their data is collected from and managed by the accompanying adult, who is responsible for consent on their behalf.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt action to delete it.

13 — Changes to This Policy

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal obligations. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email.

We encourage you to review this Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

14 — Third-Party Links

External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites — for example, accommodation partners, destination guides, or social media platforms. These sites operate under their own privacy policies, and we have no control over or responsibility for their content or data practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit before providing personal information.

Privacy Enquiries

To exercise your data rights, ask a question, or raise a concern about how we handle your information, reach our team directly.
